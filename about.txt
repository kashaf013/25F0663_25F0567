----------------------- ABOUT THE GAME -----------------------

The Locker Heist is a detective-based game in which players
take on the role of an investigator and attempt to solve a
mysterious robbery.

A wealthy business owner hosted a party at their cottage.
During the event, a locker hidden behind a bookshelf in the
Study Room was robbed. Eight individuals were present at the
cottage, and one of them is responsible for the incident.

The player must investigate seven different locations and
interview the eight individuals to collect hidden letter
clues. These clues are used to complete two secret words
that help reveal information about the culprit.

The detective must carefully examine the collected clues,
manage available turns and maintain credibility throughout
the investigation. The main objective is to identify the
correct culprit before running out of turns or credibility.

The game combines problem-solving and investigation with
Object Oriented Programming concepts to provide an
interactive console-based gaming experience.
