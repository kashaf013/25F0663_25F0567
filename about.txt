-------------- ABOUT THE GAME --------------
A wealthy business owner hosted a party at their cottage.
During the party, a locker hidden behind a bookshelf in
the Study Room was robbed. Eight people were present and
one of them is the culprit.

The detective visits seven locations and interviews eight
people to collect letter clues, reveal two words, and identify
the culprit before credibility runs out.
