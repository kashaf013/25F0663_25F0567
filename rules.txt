============================================================
                    THE LOCKER HEIST
                       GAME RULES
============================================================

1. GAME START
   - Each detective starts with 100 credibility points.
   - A total of 25 turns are available to complete the investigation.

2. INVESTIGATION
   - The detective must investigate the available locations
     and collect letter clues.
   - The collected letters are used to complete two secret
     words for identifying the culprit.

3. CREDIBILITY SYSTEM
   - Each wrong turn reduces credibility by 5 points.
   - Each incorrect word reduces credibility by 10 points.
   - The detective must manage credibility carefully during
     the investigation.

4. WINNING CONDITION
   - The detective wins by correctly identifying and
     accusing the culprit.

5. LOSING CONDITION
   - The game ends in a loss if credibility reaches zero.
   - The game also ends if all 25 turns are used before
     identifying the correct culprit.

6. FINAL OBJECTIVE
   - Investigate the cottage, collect the required clues,
     complete both secret words and identify the culprit
     before the available turns or credibility run out.

============================================================
                    END OF GAME RULES
============================================================
